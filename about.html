What this site is

StaticQuasar931 is a mix of an unblocked games hub, a curated browser game collection, and an evolving project space. Some parts are focused on helping people find games more easily, some parts are focused on organizing content better, and some parts are focused on testing new ideas and original projects.

What makes it different

The goal is not just to dump random links everywhere. The goal is to keep improving the site, organize content better, expand the collection, and make it easier to discover games, genres, pages, and fun side projects.

The Google Sites side contains a larger collection with over 800 games, while the GitHub Pages side works as a supporting hub, archive, discovery layer, and project space.
