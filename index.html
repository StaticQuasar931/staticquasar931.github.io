How I Build

I update pages to make things smoother, bigger, and clearer. My goal is not just to put something online, it is to make things easier and better for everyone. My website is built to be understandable, efficient, and worth revisiting.

Design philosophy

I focus on ease, speed, and usability, making sure everything runs how it should. I make pages and games that work in all environments, whether it is home, school, or work.

I value games having shortcuts and ease for everyone. One simple example is controls. Whenever it is possible, I strive to support both arrow keys and WASD so more people can use a project the way that feels most natural to them. This is just one way to prove my commitment to everyone.

I also care about visual clarity. Strong structure, image assets, spacing, better labels, and cleaner layout choices help people understand what they are seeing and what to do next.

This page links directly to the image asset collection I use because I want the visual side of the work to be visible too, not hidden behind the final result.
